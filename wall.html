Crew wall

Post your GMX trades for the crew. Paste the share link GMX gives you and your trade card goes up on the wall.

To get a share link on GMX, open a position or a closed trade, tap Share, and copy the link. The card shows whatever GMX shows, including your own referral code if you have one. Tapping a card on this wall opens GMX with the crew code.
